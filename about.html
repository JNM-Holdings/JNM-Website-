About us

Founded in 2010 and headquartered in the Blue Zone of Gigiri, Nairobi.

Our story

Real estate services built for local and multinational clients.

JNM Holdings Limited was born from the need to provide efficient, modern and international-standard real estate services in the Gigiri embassy community. Today it is among the leading preferred real estate and property developers in the area.

Our integrated, team-based approach supports effective, responsive communication, and our proactive approach to management keeps the operational and financial performance of your property our highest priority.

With a team of highly skilled and experienced professionals, we continue to build on repeat clients and tenants. Alongside new leases, lease renewals, urban developments and construction, we manage and maintain office centers, diplomatic chanceries, ambassadorial residences, convention and shopping centers, and hospitality centers.

Geographic coverage

Where our assets are
